## 9. Presentation Guidelines

During the final presentation:

1. Speak slowly and clearly.
2. Pause between major sections.
3. Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
4. Explain the business value along with the technical implementation.
5. Maintain eye contact during a live presentation.
6. Emphasize important AI outputs.
7. Keep the live demo within the planned 10-minute window.
8. Have backup screenshots of important outputs.
9. Be prepared to explain limitations honestly.
10. Do not claim that simulated results are production-validated results.

## 10. Final Demo Script

"Zecpath AI is designed to solve one of the major challenges in modern
hiring — scalability and efficient candidate evaluation.

Instead of relying entirely on manual screening, Zecpath AI automates
multiple stages of the hiring process, starting from resume analysis and
continuing through screening, interviews, technical evaluation and final
decision support.

Let me walk you through a real example."

### Resume Stage

"First, we upload the candidate's resume. The system extracts relevant
candidate information and evaluates the resume against the job
requirements."

### ATS Stage

"Next, the ATS engine calculates the candidate's job-fit score based on
the available candidate and job information."

### Screening Stage

"The candidate then goes through AI-based screening, where their responses
are evaluated."

### HR Interview

"The HR interview stage evaluates communication, confidence and relevant
behavioral signals."

### Technical Evaluation

"The technical evaluation measures the candidate's technical knowledge
and practical performance."

### Final Decision

"These evaluation results are combined to generate a structured final
score and hiring decision."

### Hiring Report

"Finally, recruiters receive a structured report containing the candidate's
score, decision, strengths, weaknesses and relevant risk indicators."

### Closing

"This demonstrates how Zecpath AI can provide a structured,
scalable and intelligent approach to the hiring process."
