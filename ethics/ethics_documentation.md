# Zecpath AI Ethics & Compliance Framework

## Objective

Ensure AI-driven hiring remains fair, transparent,
and privacy-focused.

---

## Ethical Principles

### Fairness

- No gender bias
- No age bias
- No religion bias
- No caste bias
- No location bias
- Only job-related attributes considered

---

### Transparency

- Explainable scoring
- Recruiters receive score breakdown
- Human review possible

---

### Accountability

- Every decision logged
- Audit history maintained

---

### Privacy

- Secure data storage
- Candidate information masked
- Limited data retention

---

## Consent Requirements

Candidates must agree to

- AI evaluation
- Data processing
- Audio recording (optional)

---

## Data Retention

Resume : Stored

Interview : Stored

Audio : Optional

Retention : 90 Days

Deletion : Automatic / Manual Request

---

## Compliance

✔ GDPR Partial Alignment

✔ Consent Management

✔ Data Minimization

✔ Right to Erasure

✔ Secure APIs

✔ Audit Logging
