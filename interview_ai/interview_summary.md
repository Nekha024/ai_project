# Interview Summary Generator – Zecpath AI

## Objective

Convert AI interview evaluations into recruiter-friendly summaries.

## Components

- HR Score Analysis
- Communication Analysis
- Behavioral Analysis
- Cultural Fit Detection
- Decision Generation
- Natural Language Summary

## Decision Rules

| Score | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| 75+ | Strong Hire |
| 55-74 | Consider |
| <55 | Reject |

## Output

- Candidate ID
- Overall Score
- Decision
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Risks
- Inconsistencies
- Cultural Fit
- Natural Language Summary
