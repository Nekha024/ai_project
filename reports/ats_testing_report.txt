Testing Methodology

1. Resume uploaded into ATS system

3. ATS score generated

--------------------------------------------------

Observations

Strengths:
- High accuracy for technical roles
- Strong semantic matching
- Consistent candidate ranking

Weaknesses:
- Lower accuracy in non-tech roles
- Soft skills extraction needs improvement
- Some keyword dependency still exists

--------------------------------------------------

Conclusion

Overall ATS performance is reliable for technical hiring workflows.
Further improvements are required for non-technical domains and soft-skill understanding.
